# Official System Branding & Acronym Proposals (Replacing Tentative "DASH")

**Document Purpose:** Three (3) original, standalone system name and acronym proposals developed for the **DA-CALABARZON Interactive 3D Research Station Mapping, Wayfinding & Agricultural Plot Intelligence Platform**.  
**Prepared For:** DA CALABARZON (Region IV-A) Tech Update Presentation

---

## Naming Strategy & Criteria

Each of the three proposals below was designed around seven core branding principles:
1. **Originality:** Fresh, independently developed names that do not copy existing commercial software or institutional titles.
2. **Meaningful Acronym:** Every letter represents a real capability of the system—from 3D building visualization and pedestrian routing to agricultural plot and tree inventory tracking.
3. **Standalone Identity:** Functions as an independent software brand without locking the name to a single station (so it represents **DA-LARES**, **DA-QARES**, **DA-RARES**, **DA-CARES**, and future stations equally).
4. **Professionalism:** Credible, modern, and suited for an official Department of Agriculture digital platform.
5. **Memorability:** Easy to pronounce, spell, recognize, and remember.
6. **Relevance:** Communicates that the system serves **both campus visitors** (wayfinding to buildings and offices) **and station workers/researchers** (knowing what is planted in each agricultural plot, tree counts, and annual vs. monthly crop cycles).
7. **Distinctiveness:** Avoids generic combinations of overused tech words.

## Proposal 1: TERRAVIS

* **Proposed System Name:** **T**hree-dimensional **E**state **R**outing, **R**esearch **A**tlas, and **V**egetative **I**nventory **S**ystem
* **Acronym:** **TERRAVIS** *(pronounced "teh-RAH-vis" or "TAIR-uh-vis")*
* **Acronym Breakdown:**
  * **T — Three-dimensional:** Represents the custom-modeled 3D architectural buildings (under 300 KB each), 3D terrain, and orbital camera perspectives.
  * **E — Estate:** Refers to the multi-hectare agricultural research station compounds and facilities across the region.
  * **R — Routing:** Captures the turn-by-turn pedestrian pathfinding, animated walkthroughs, and live smartphone GPS guidance.
  * **R — Research:** Highlights the core scientific mandate of the experiment stations, laboratories, and trial sites.
  * **A — Atlas:** Signifies a comprehensive, publicly searchable multi-station geospatial directory and high-resolution drone orthomosaic map.
  * **V — Vegetative:** Directly reflects the living agricultural plots, orchards, screenhouses, and seasonal crop trials.
  * **I — Inventory:** Speaks to the worker-facing agronomic tracking of planted crop varieties, exact tree counts, and annual versus monthly plot cycles.
  * **S — System:** Denotes a unified, integrated software platform across public web, lobby kiosk, and mobile devices.
* **Meaning and Rationale:**
  Derived from the Latin roots *Terra* (earth, land, and soil) and *Vis* (vision and sight), **TERRAVIS** literally means *"seeing the land clearly."* It bridges the two halves of the platform: giving visitors a clear 3D visual guide to navigate buildings and offices, while giving station workers a living atlas and vegetative inventory of what is planted across every experimental plot.
* **Brand Identity:**
  Authoritative, scientific, and forward-looking. **TERRAVIS** sounds like a flagship geospatial and agritech platform capable of scaling across all research stations while remaining immediately intuitive to both agricultural scientists and the general public.

## Proposal 2: VERDANT

* **Proposed System Name:** **V**isual **E**nvironment for **R**outing, **D**irectory **A**ccess, and **N**ursery-to-plot **T**racking
* **Acronym:** **VERDANT** *(pronounced "VUR-dnt")*
* **Acronym Breakdown:**
  * **V — Visual:** Represents the high-resolution drone orthomosaics, optimized WebP facility photography, and *"How to Spot It"* visual landmark guides.
  * **E — Environment:** Refers to the interactive 3D digital twin of each research station's buildings, roads, and agricultural landscapes.
  * **R — Routing:** Represents the shortest-path pedestrian navigation, QR handoff, and live compass-assisted mobile GPS.
  * **D — Directory:** Covers the searchable registry of numbered buildings, internal offices, laboratories, conference halls, and gates.
  * **A — Access:** Reflects public online searchability by anyone, anywhere, alongside on-site touchscreen kiosk accessibility.
  * **N — Nursery-to-plot:** Captures the full agricultural research lifecycle—from tissue culture laboratories and screenhouses (`Plots 17–18`) out to open field trial plots and orchards (`Plots 1–45`).
  * **T — Tracking:** Highlights the operational monitoring of field plots for station workers, including tree counts, current planted varieties, and annual versus monthly cropping schedules.
* **Meaning and Rationale:**
  In English, *"verdant"* means lush, green, and thriving with cultivated vegetation—mirroring the signature emerald-and-gold visual theme of the interface and the living orchards and trial plots of the research stations. Every letter in the acronym maps directly to a real feature of the system without filler words.
* **Brand Identity:**
  Vibrant, approachable, and unmistakably agricultural. **VERDANT** is a single, memorable dictionary word that is effortless to pronounce and spell, projecting growth, sustainability, and smart digital modernization.

## Proposal 3: ARBORIS

* **Proposed System Name:** **A**gronomic **R**oute-finding, **B**uilding **O**rientation, and **R**esearch-plot **I**nformation **S**ystem
* **Acronym:** **ARBORIS** *(pronounced "ahr-BOHR-is")*
* **Acronym Breakdown:**
  * **A — Agronomic:** Grounds the system in agricultural science, crop production, and research station operations.
  * **R — Route-finding:** Describes the automated pedestrian pathfinding, landmark turn instructions, and mobile GPS wayfinding.
  * **B — Building:** Represents the custom-modeled 3D campus structures, laboratories, storage facilities, and administrative offices.
  * **O — Orientation:** Captures how the system orients visitors and staff in 3D space using inspection orbits, compass headings, and X-ray building transparency.
  * **R — Research-plot:** Highlights the 45 experimental field plots, 23 commodity zones, and screenhouse sub-plots mapped across the station.
  * **I — Information:** Refers to both visitor-facing office/service details and worker-facing plot data (planted crops, tree counts, and annual vs. monthly cycles).
  * **S — System:** Signifies a cohesive, multi-station digital platform.
* **Meaning and Rationale:**
  Inspired by *Arbor* (trees and orchards) and *Orbis* (world, sphere, and orbital map), **ARBORIS** honors the deep heritage of orchard and crop research (citrus, coffee, mango, cacao, makapuno, and seasonal field crops) while directly stating its three core functions in the expanded title: **Route-finding**, **Building Orientation**, and **Research-plot Information**.
* **Brand Identity:**
  Crisp, institutional, and precision-engineered. **ARBORIS** carries the poise of an official government research and spatial intelligence system while remaining distinctive and easy to remember as a standalone brand.
