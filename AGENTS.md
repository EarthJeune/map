## 🏛️ Project Summary & Architecture
- **Application:** High-performance 3D interactive campus wayfinding system for the Department of Agriculture - Southern Tagalog Integrated Agricultural Research Center (DA-LARES) in Lipa City, Batangas.
- **Runtime Targets:**
  - Portrait touch kiosk totem (Raspberry Pi / Linux / Chrome kiosk mode, `index.html`).
  - Mobile web browser via QR code navigation.
- **Tech Stack:**
  - Vanilla HTML5 / Modern Modular JavaScript (ES Modules in `js/`).
  - Mapbox GL JS with custom Three.js GLTF 3D building layers.
  - A* graph-based road & walkway pathfinding (`roads.json`, `js/pathfinding.js`).
  - Local HTTP server running at `http://localhost:8080/` via Node.js (`server.js`).
  - Headless Blender 4 automation listening on TCP `127.0.0.1:9876`.

## 🎯 Active Strategic Directive: Authentic 3D Campus Modeling
The campus is undergoing a progressive upgrade from flat extruded procedural boxes to **authentic, bespoke 3D architectural models** built from ground-truth field photographs located in `Building Images/<Building_Name>/`.

### 🚨 Strict Rules for 3D Building Integration:
1. **Strictly One Building at a Time:** Model, export, register, and verify each building individually. Do NOT batch-model multiple buildings without user confirmation.
2. **Performance Budget:** Every GLB model in `models/*.glb` MUST stay under **$300\text{ KB}$** to ensure instant rendering without web crashes or mobile lag.
3. **No Autonomous Browser Subagent:** Do NOT spawn a browser subagent unless explicitly instructed by the user.
4. **Strictly No Extraneous Outdoor Props:** Do NOT model external lawns, terrain turf slabs, roads, driveways, curbs, streetlamps, vehicles, or trees outside the building footprint. Keep models strictly confined to the authentic building structure (foundation, walls, roof, doors, windows, attached signage, canopies, attached steps/stoops) to prevent visual clutter and clipping with adjacent campus roads and lots.
5. **Ground-Truth Entrance Alignment via `roads.json`:** Always inspect the building's entrance coordinate in `roads.json` (`features[].properties.type === 'entrance' && features[].properties.buildingId === '<building_id>'`). The modeled main entrance/front facade MUST orient directly toward this plotted `roads.json` entrance node.
6. **Strictly Ground-Truth Offices & Standalone Types:** NEVER invent, fabricate, or assume offices or internal units for any building. Only buildings explicitly provided by the user have offices (`tanco_hall_building`, `admin_building`, `ored_building`, `lares_building`, `raed_building`, `multi_purpose_administrative_building`, `rnd_building`, `rsbsa_building`, `mis_rafis_building`). All other buildings are standalone facilities or pending official office assignments, and MUST NOT have any entries in `BUILDING_OFFICES` in [js/data/offices.js](file:///c:/Users/LGU-SanPedro/Downloads/map/js/data/offices.js). Standalone buildings (such as Argosino Hall) must never be labeled as 'Office Building'.
7. **Category Filters Bar is for QARES Standalone Only:** The horizontal category filter chips bar (`.kiosk-categories-bar`) must NEVER be displayed in the LARES campus map directory. Facility filtering in DA-LARES is conducted via the dedicated **Type Filter Dropdown** next to the search bar across the 7 verified categories (`infrastructure`, `trial_sites`, `orchard`, `office`, `laboratory`, `conference`, `digital_agri`).
8. **Screenhouse Master Zone & Sub-Plot Hierarchy:**
   - Screenhouses in Plot 17 (Screenhouses 1, 2, 3, 4, 5) are officially sub-plots of **TC Makapuno & Banana Screenhouse** (`zone_tc_makapuno_screenhouse`).
   - Screenhouses in Plot 18 (Screenhouses 6, 7, 8, 9) are officially sub-plots of **Coffee & Citrus Screenhouse** (`zone_coffee_citrus_screenhouse`).
9. **Strictly Ground-Truth Agricultural Plots & Zero Sub-Plot Inventions:** NEVER invent, fabricate, or assume sub-plot names, crops, accessions, or internal descriptions for any field plot. Follow ONLY the 23 official zone names and plot numbers explicitly provided by the user. Multi-plot field zones (e.g. Citrus Orchard Plots 6–12) MUST NOT display any fabricated sub-plots list in the drawer panel (`subPlots: []`). Only official screenhouses explicitly named by the user in Plot 17 and Plot 18 have sub-plots, and only using the user's exact facility names without any fabricated details.
10. **Strictly WebP Photos for Map & Facility Drawers:** All campus map drawer photos and facility images in `images/` MUST be in `.webp` format. Never leave uncompressed JPG/PNG photos in `images/`. Always convert to WebP using `tools/convert_photos_to_webp.py` (max width 1440px, quality 80) to save space, accelerate mobile/kiosk asset loading, and prevent lag.

## ⚠️ Known Gotchas & Architectural Guards

- **Model Orientation & Mapbox Rotation Formula:** In Blender, buildings are modeled with the front entrance facade along $-Y$ (which points South at rotation $0^\circ$). In Mapbox GL JS, `model-rotation: [0, 0, rot]` rotates clockwise around $Z$. Therefore, if the entrance compass bearing is $\theta$:
  - When the entrance is on the South side ($\theta \approx 180^\circ$): `rotation` in Mapbox is $(\theta - 180^\circ) \approx 0^\circ$ (e.g. PGRC: $0.0^\circ$, AMAD: $341.0^\circ / -19^\circ$).
  - When the entrance is on the North side ($\theta \approx 0^\circ$): `rotation` in Mapbox is $(\theta + 180^\circ) \approx 180^\circ$ to $200^\circ$ (e.g. Canteen: $198.5^\circ$, Tanco: $198.9^\circ$, RAED: $195.9^\circ$, Guard House: $180.0^\circ$).
  - **CRITICAL:** Never set `rotation: 180.0°` for a South-facing entrance! That reverses the building 180° away from the entrance node.
- **Occlusion Transparency for 3D GLB Models:** Custom 3D buildings use a dual-layer model architecture (`3d-buildings-models-layer` solid at `1.0` vs `3d-buildings-models-transparent` frosted glass at `0.25`), because Mapbox GL JS forbids data-driven expressions on `model-opacity` for GeoJSON sources. When the walker passes a building (proximity $\le 22\text{ m}$ centroid / $15\text{ m}$ perimeter) or the building occludes the camera sightline corridor, [js/occlusion.js](file:///c:/Users/LGU-SanPedro/Downloads/map/js/occlusion.js) dynamically filters the building from the solid layer into the translucent layer and restores it when clear.
- **Occlusion Transparency Bug:** Never allow procedural extruded boxes to pop up when clicking custom 3D buildings. [js/occlusion.js](file:///c:/Users/LGU-SanPedro/Downloads/map/js/occlusion.js) preserves module-level `BASE_NO_3D_MODEL_FILTER` (`['!=', ['get', 'has_3d_model'], true]`) across all extruded filters in `applyOcclusionFilters()` and `resetOcclusion()`.
- **Blender Execution Scope:** When executing code via `run_in_blender.py`, `__name__` is NOT `'__main__'`. Call the build function directly at module level.
- **Drone Orthomosaic Georeferencing Offset & Calibration:** The high-resolution offline drone imagery (`LARES v1.mbtiles` served at `/api/tiles/{z}/{x}/{y}.png`) possesses a slight photogrammetric georeferencing offset relative to global Google Satellite WGS84 datum. An interactive calibration control (`js/drone-calibration.js`) is integrated into Admin Mode (`Alt + Shift + A` or `?admin=1`). It provides real-time X/Y range sliders, D-Pad buttons, and keyboard arrow keys to nudge the 3D models, 2D drawings, markers, and trees simultaneously. The calibrated offset `(dx, dy)` in meters is persisted to `localStorage` and `/api/drone-offset` (`drone_offset.json`). By default, it auto-applies exclusively when "Aerial Drone View" is selected, ensuring that Google Maps/Mapbox 3D views remain 100% untouched.

  - **Fill-Rate & DPR Clamping:** Older Intel HD/UHD graphics (HD 4000/4400/4600/520/620) are bottlenecked by fill rate when rendered at high `devicePixelRatio`. [js/performance.js](file:///c:/Users/LGU-SanPedro/Downloads/map/js/performance.js) automatically detects Intel integrated GPUs and locks `pixelRatio` to `1.0` (or adapts if sustained FPS drops below 38 FPS), raising frame rates from ~25-30 FPS to **55-60 FPS**.
  - **Thermal & 24/7 Idle Power Control:** Unthrottled 60 FPS 360° idle rotation causes thermal throttling in fanless/sealed kiosks.
