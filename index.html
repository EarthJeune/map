Welcome to iGUIDE

Interactive Geospatial Unified Information Directory and Explorer

What is iGUIDE?
The official interactive 3D campus directory and wayfinding system connecting Department of Agriculture CALABARZON research stations — DA-LARES, DA-QARES, DA-RARES, and DA-CARES.
What does our map do?
Explore authentic 3D campus architecture and aerial drone views, search buildings, research divisions, offices, laboratories, warehouses, greenhouses, screenhouses, automated weather stations, and agricultural plots, and get real-time turn-by-turn walking routes.
Touch Kiosk & Mobile QR Navigation
Browse facilities and preview routes on the on-site touch kiosk, scan the QR code to open the interactive 3D map or hand off your route to your phone, or use live GPS turn-by-turn walking guidance directly on your mobile device.
